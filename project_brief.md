# Project Brief: GRURU MUSEUM
**Theme:** "New Knowledge. New Storytelling. Built for Everyday Living."

## 1. Project Overview & Core Concept
- **Brand Identity:** GRURU MUSEUM - A digital knowledge museum transforming human wisdom into engaging narratives.
- **Core Belief:** "Knowledge hidden in the past still holds immense value for life today—it simply needs to be excavated, decoded, and retold."
- **Creative Concept:** **Future Archaeology** ("ห้องแล็บของนักโบราณคดีจากอนาคต" - The laboratory of archaeologists from the future studying human civilization like ancient artifacts).
- **Aesthetic Tension:** Modern Art Gallery calm meets Sci-Fi Laboratory cutting-edge tech.
- **Dual Knowledge Graph Products:**
  1. **Data Booming:** Guided exploratory experience. A single seed topic explodes outwards into concentric layers of interconnected knowledge.
  2. **Interactive Graph:** Full open-ended network map where visitors filter, zoom, and investigate cross-era relationships.
- **Three Pillar Promises:**
  - *New Knowledge:* Rediscovered, verifiable, source-attributed wisdom.
  - *New Storytelling:* Visual, interactive, and bite-sized instead of dense prose.
  - *Built for Everyday Living:* Every exhibit culminates in an actionable takeaway.

## 2. Vision & 5-Step Mission Pipeline
- **Vision:** "Reviving hidden knowledge from the past as new stories, built for everyday living." (ปลุกความรู้ซึ่งซ่อนอยู่ในอดีตให้กลับมามีชีวิต ในรูปแบบเรื่องเล่าใหม่ที่คนรุ่นใหม่ใช้ได้จริงทุกวัน)
- **5-Step Mission (The Knowledge Pipeline):**
  1. **Excavate (ขุดค้น):** Sift history, science, and folklore; preserve strict citations.
  2. **Decode (ถอดรหัส):** Convert unstructured data into structured graph nodes & relations.
  3. **Retell (เล่าใหม่):** Visual storytelling format readable within minutes.
  4. **Connect (เชื่อมโยง):** Open datasets via Data Booming & Interactive Graph.
  5. **Apply (นำไปใช้):** End with "Everyday Takeaway" cards for immediate daily application.

## 3. Brand Positioning & Persona
- **Positioning Statement:** "สำหรับคนวัย 22–40 ปีที่อยากเข้าใจโลกให้ลึกกว่าคลิปสั้น แต่ไม่อยากรู้สึกเหมือนนั่งเรียน GRURU MUSEUM คือพิพิธภัณฑ์ความรู้ดิจิทัลสไตล์ modern art ผสม sci-fi ที่เปลี่ยนความรู้จากอดีตให้เป็นกราฟที่สำรวจได้ด้วยตัวเองและเป็นเรื่องเล่าที่ใช้ได้จริง"
- **Brand Essence:** "Past is Data, Future is Story" (อดีตคือข้อมูล อนาคตคือเรื่องเล่า).
- **Brand Archetype:** Future Curator (ภัณฑารักษ์จากอนาคต) - Brilliant yet unpretentious, playful yet strictly evidentiary.
- **Target Audience:**
  1. *Curious Starter (22–27 yrs):* Mobile-first, visual-driven, 3–5 min takeaways, 1080x1350 social share cards.
  2. *Creative Professional (25–34 yrs):* Deep graph exploration, saved collections, downloadable datasets for work inspiration.
  3. *Lifelong Learner (30–40 yrs):* Credible depth, comfortable reading, clear practical life application.

## 4. Visual Identity & Color System ("Past Warm × Future Electric")
- **Base Canvas (70%):**
  - Void Black `#0B0B0F` (Dark mode canvas)
  - Archive Paper `#F2EDE3` (Warm museum archival paper, text & light mode)
- **Structure (20%):**
  - Lab Graphite `#17171F` (HUD Panels, Card surfaces)
  - Grid Line `#2C2C3A` (Coordinate rules & 48px graph grid)
  - Muted Fog `#8A8A9E` (Subtitles, metadata)
- **Brand Accents (<10%):**
  - Specimen Lime `#D7FF3A` (Primary CTA, active selected node, focal glow)
  - Ember Orange `#FF5A1F` (Past, era tags, hard drop shadow `4px 4px 0 #FF5A1F`)
  - Quantum Violet `#8C6BFF` (Technology, future horizons)
  - Hologram Cyan `#2EE6FF` (Data, science, network edges)
- **Signature Gradient:** `Time Warp` = `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF5A1F, #8C6BFF)`
- **6 Knowledge Domains (Color & Geometric Shape Code):**
  1. Science: Cyan `#2EE6FF` [Circle]
  2. History: Ember `#FF5A1F` [Hexagon]
  3. Technology: Violet `#8C6BFF` [Square]
  4. Culture & Wisdom: Amber `#FFC233` [Triangle]
  5. Nature & Life: Bio Green `#34E89E` [Diamond]
  6. Art & Design: Coral `#FF5FA2` [Ring]

## 5. Typography & UI Signatures
- **Display / Headers:** Chakra Petch (Geometric, Sci-Fi)
- **Body / Documentation:** IBM Plex Sans Thai (Clear, Scientific, Elegant)
- **Metadata / Coordinates / Code:** IBM Plex Mono (Museum Specimen Index)
- **Specimen Label Format:** `GRM-[YEAR]-[CATEGORY]-[INDEX]` (e.g. `GRM-1872-SCI-014`)
- **Card Geometry:** Chamfered corners (12px 45° cut corners on top-left and bottom-right)
- **Motion Rhythm:** Scan → Decode → Connect (150ms micro, 300ms UI, 600-1000ms narrative)
